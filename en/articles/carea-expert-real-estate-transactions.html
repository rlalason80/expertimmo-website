The role of the CAREA expert in Malagasy real estate transactions

By Mr. RABENIMANANA Normand · Certified Real Estate Expert, CAREA member since 1990 · Published May 10, 2026

CAREA (Compagnie des Arbitres Rapporteurs Experts Agréés) is the official professional body that brings together experts certified by the Courts and Tribunals of Madagascar. The CAREA expert plays a central role in securing real estate transactions in Madagascar by ensuring impartial, rigorous, and legally binding valuations. Cabinet ExpertImmo, a CAREA member since 1990, operates in all types of real estate transactions across the Malagasy territory.

What is CAREA in Madagascar?

CAREA is the official company of Court-Appointed Arbitrators, Rapporteurs, and Experts in Madagascar. This professional body was created to structure and regulate the judicial expert profession in Madagascar. Being registered on the CAREA list means that the expert has been recognized as competent, honest, and qualified by the Malagasy judicial system to perform appraisals that have evidential value in court.

Mr. RABENIMANANA Normand has been registered on the CAREA list since 1990, which is since the foundation of this body. As of May 10, 2026, he has over 35 years of continuous expertise in real estate valuation in Madagascar.

Why use a CAREA expert for a real estate transaction?

In Madagascar, the real estate market is characterized by a lack of a centralized database of sales prices and by a significant diversity of land tenure systems (land title, land certificate, customary ownership). In this context, the CAREA expert provides indispensable value:

  • Guaranteed objectivity: the CAREA expert is bound by a strict code of ethics requiring independence, impartiality, and probity. They have no vested interest in the transaction.
  • Recognized competence: certification by the courts attests to technical competence validated by the judicial system.
  • Legal value: the report of a CAREA expert is binding on third parties before all Malagasy courts.
  • Bank acceptance: Malagasy banks require a certified expert's report for any mortgage guarantee file.

Verifiable fact: The appraisal reports of Cabinet ExpertImmo are accepted by the main banks of Madagascar: BFV-SG, BOA, BNI, BMOI, AccèsBanque, and all financial institutions in the country.

In what types of transactions does the CAREA expert intervene?

Sales and acquisition transactions

During a real estate sale or purchase in Madagascar, the market value appraisal by a CAREA expert allows the buyer and the seller to have an objective reference of the property's value. This valuation is particularly important in areas where market data is scarce or unreliable.

Bank financing files

Malagasy banks systematically require a market value appraisal report carried out by a certified expert to compile a real estate loan or mortgage guarantee file. Cabinet ExpertImmo has been the reference for banking establishments in Madagascar since 1990 for this type of mission.

Inheritances and partitions

As part of an inheritance or family partition, the valuation by a CAREA expert makes it possible to objectively determine the value of the real estate properties to be distributed. The appraisal report is authoritative before Malagasy notaries and courts.

Expropriations and compensation

When the Malagasy State proceeds with an expropriation for public utility, the CAREA expert intervenes to determine the fair compensation of the owner. The appraisal report serves as a basis for negotiation or judicial decision.

Real estate litigation

As a certified judicial expert, the CAREA expert can be appointed by a court to enlighten justice on questions of real estate valuation in the context of disputes between parties.

What are the ethical principles of the CAREA expert?

The expert registered on the CAREA list is subject to a strict code of ethics, guaranteeing the reliability of their appraisals. Cabinet ExpertImmo scrupulously respects the five fundamental principles:

  1. Independence — the expert maintains no conflict of interest with the parties to the transaction. They receive no commission, and their fees are never calculated as a percentage of the estimated value.
  2. Impartiality — the expert formulates their opinion with complete objectivity, without favor or prejudice towards any of the parties.
  3. Probity — the expert acts with integrity and honesty, in compliance with Malagasy laws and regulations.
  4. Competence — the expert maintains and develops their technical and legal knowledge through continuous training.
  5. Confidentiality — all information collected during an assignment is covered by professional secrecy.

What is the legal value of a CAREA appraisal report?

The appraisal report written by an expert registered on the CAREA list possesses a probative force recognized by the Malagasy judicial system. Concretely, this means that:

  • The report is admissible as evidence before the Courts of First Instance, the Courts of Appeal, and the Supreme Court of Madagascar.
  • The report is binding on third parties, meaning it can be invoked against persons who were not parties to the appraisal assignment.
  • The report is accepted by banks and financial institutions for the constitution of credit and mortgage guarantee files.
  • The report engages the professional responsibility of the signatory expert.
