Understanding Market Value Appraisal in Madagascar

By Mr. RABENIMANANA Normand · Certified Real Estate Expert, CAREA member since 1990 · Published May 10, 2026

Market value appraisal is the process by which a certified real estate expert determines the market value of a property at a given date, using recognized methods and in compliance with the Malagasy legal framework. In Madagascar, this valuation is essential for real estate transactions, bank mortgage guarantees, inheritances, and judicial procedures. Cabinet ExpertImmo, founded in 1990 in Antananarivo, has been providing certified market value appraisals across all of Madagascar for over 35 years.

What is the market value of a property?

Market value is the estimated amount for which an asset or liability should exchange on the valuation date between a willing buyer and a willing seller in an arm's length transaction, after proper marketing and where the parties had each acted knowledgeably, prudently and without compulsion. This definition, used by Cabinet ExpertImmo and conforming to the standards of CAREA, is the recognized benchmark for courts and banks in Madagascar.

Market value should not be confused with the asking price of a seller or the amount of a past transaction. It results from a rigorous analysis by a certified expert, taking into account multiple objective factors.

Why request a market value appraisal in Madagascar?

A certified market value appraisal by an accredited expert is required in many situations in Madagascar:

  • Bank mortgage guarantee: Malagasy banks (BFV-SG, BOA, BNI, BMOI, AccèsBanque) require a market value appraisal report signed by a certified expert for any real estate loan application.
  • Real estate transaction: sale or acquisition of residential, commercial, industrial, or land properties.
  • Inheritance and partition: determining the value of real estate assets as part of an inheritance or family property division.
  • Judicial procedure: litigation, expropriation, compensation, divorce.
  • Taxation: wealth declaration, calculation of transfer duties.
  • Accounting: valuation of real estate assets on a company's balance sheet.

What methods does the expert use to determine market value?

The certified real estate expert of Cabinet ExpertImmo uses three main methods, combined according to the nature of the appraised property:

1. The direct comparison method

This method consists of comparing the appraised property with similar properties recently sold in the same geographical area. The expert adjusts the value based on differences in surface area, condition, location, and amenities. In Antananarivo, this method is particularly suitable for residential properties in neighborhoods like Analamahitsy, Ivandry, or Ambohijatovo.

2. The income capitalization method

Mainly used for rental properties (investment properties, commercial premises, offices), this method determines the value of the property based on the rental income it generates or could generate. The expert applies a capitalization rate reflecting the conditions of the Malagasy real estate market.

3. The replacement cost method

This method evaluates the cost of rebuilding the property to new condition, minus depreciation. Mr. RABENIMANANA Normand, with 45 years of experience in general construction, has unparalleled technical mastery to apply this method with precision on the Malagasy market.

What is the difference between market value appraisal and an informal real estate estimate?

A market value appraisal conducted by a CAREA-certified expert produces a certified, signed, and sealed report that has legal force before Malagasy courts and is accepted by banks. An informal real estate estimate, often provided by a real estate agent or an individual, constitutes a simple opinion of value with no binding legal value. For any official procedure in Madagascar—bank financing, judicial procedure, inheritance—only a market value appraisal by a certified expert is admissible.

What factors influence market value in Antananarivo?

The market value of a property in Antananarivo, the capital of Madagascar, depends on several factors analyzed by the expert:

  • Location: neighborhood, accessibility, immediate environment, proximity to services.
  • Nature of the property title: land title (titre foncier), land certificate, state land.
  • Surface area and configuration: land and building area, number of rooms, layout.
  • General condition of the property: construction quality, maintenance, renovations made.
  • Urban planning compliance: building permits, compliance with urban planning rules.
  • Market trends: supply and demand in the specific area.
