Why does Madagascar attract real estate investors?

Madagascar, the world's fourth-largest island, has a diversified real estate portfolio and acquisition prices generally lower than those of the real estate markets in Southern Africa or the Indian Ocean. The capital Antananarivo concentrates a significant part of the real estate demand, but other cities like Toamasina, Mahajanga, and Nosy Be also attract investors, particularly in the tourism and hotel sectors.

Real estate investment sectors in Madagascar include luxury residential properties, commercial and office spaces, industrial land, agricultural estates, and tourism projects. Each type of property presents specific valuation requirements that only a certified expert can properly understand.

What are the specific risks of the Malagasy real estate market?

The real estate market in Madagascar presents several risks that the investor must identify and manage:

  • Land complexity: Madagascar has several coexisting land tenure systems (land title, land certificate, untitled customary ownership). Verifying the legal status of the property is a critical step that the real estate expert systematically performs.
  • Lack of a centralized database: unlike other markets, Madagascar does not have a public registry of real estate transactions. The certified expert uses their knowledge of the local market, accumulated over more than 35 years in the case of Cabinet ExpertImmo, to establish reliable comparisons.
  • Overvaluation of properties: without an independent appraisal, the investor risks paying a price higher than the real value of the property. Market value appraisal by a CAREA expert provides an objective reference.
  • Urban planning risks: non-compliance with urban planning rules, lack of a building permit, undeclared servitudes. The expert verifies the property's compliance with current regulations.
  • Restrictions for foreigners: Malagasy land law imposes certain restrictions on foreign investors, particularly on land acquisition. The expert advises on the applicable legal framework.

What questions to ask before investing in real estate in Madagascar?

Before any real estate investment in Madagascar, the investor should verify the following points with a certified expert:

  1. Does the property have a proper land title or land certificate?
  2. Is the seller the legitimate owner and do they have the legal capacity to sell?
  3. Is the property free of any mortgage, servitude, or undeclared charge?
  4. Does the property comply with current urban planning rules?
  5. What is the real market value of the property according to an independent expert?
  6. What restrictions apply to foreign investors for this type of property?
