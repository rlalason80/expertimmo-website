What does a CAREA appraisal mean for you?

The reports issued by the firm comply with the code of ethics of the Compagnie des Arbitres Rapporteurs et Experts Agréés (CAREA). They carry the evidential weight attached to a CAREA-registered expert, are admissible to the credit departments of banking institutions, and are accepted by notaries as a basis for registration duties. Their effect towards third parties is assessed under the ordinary rules of law applicable to expert reports.

All engagements are conducted in accordance with the CAREA code of ethics: independence, impartiality, integrity, competence and confidentiality.

Our Areas of Practice

Judicial appraisal

Upon instruction from the Courts, the Courts of Appeal or the Supreme Court, the firm conducts expert investigations in real-estate disputes: inheritance partitions, divorces, property seizures, expropriations and construction litigation. The resulting reports are entered into the proceedings and carry the evidential weight attached to an accredited expert, the Malagasy courts retaining sole discretion to assess their scope.

Open-market valuation

Determination of the open-market value (market value) of built and unbuilt property: land, residential buildings, commercial premises, industrial buildings and hotel complexes. Methodologies in line with valuation standards: comparison approach, income-capitalisation approach and replacement-cost approach.

Bank valuation (mortgage guarantee)

Valuation of property pledged as mortgage collateral on behalf of Malagasy credit institutions. Preparation of an appraisal file that meets the requirements of credit committees and banking-supervision authorities.

Estate appraisal

Audit and advice on the valuation, management and transfer of institutional or family real-estate assets. Contradictory valuations for inheritances, gift-partitions and the liquidation of matrimonial property regimes.

Rental valuation

Determination of rental value for residential, commercial, industrial and office leases. Lease renewal, setting of eviction indemnities and rent reviews.

Cabinet ExpertImmo offers a complete range of real-estate appraisal services in Madagascar: judicial appraisal, open-market valuation, bank valuation (mortgage guarantee), estate appraisal and rental valuation. Every report is signed, sealed and carries the evidential weight attached to a CAREA-registered expert.

Rigorous expertise carried out in strict accordance with the CAREA code of ethics: independence, impartiality, competence and confidentiality.
